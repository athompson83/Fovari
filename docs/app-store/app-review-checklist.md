# Apple App Store readiness checklist

- [ ] Decide Kids Category strategy with counsel and product owners.
- [ ] Finalize privacy policy, terms, support URL, company identity, and contact channels.
- [ ] Complete App Privacy labels from the verified production data flow.
- [ ] Verify parental gate behavior against the selected age/category strategy.
- [ ] Remove or explicitly review every external link and third-party SDK.
- [ ] Implement Sign in with Apple if another qualifying third-party sign-in is offered.
- [ ] Configure Apple in-app purchase products for digital subscriptions.
- [ ] Prove purchase, restore, renewal, grace period, cancellation, and family-device behavior in
      sandbox.
- [ ] Supply a stable reviewer account and notes that explain parent/child handoff.
- [ ] Test iPhone and iPad, portrait/landscape, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and reduced motion.
- [ ] Complete age rating, encryption/export, content rights, and account deletion declarations.
- [ ] Archive signed binary, dependency inventory, screenshots, privacy evidence, and review result.

No item in this document is complete solely because the local application contains a placeholder.
