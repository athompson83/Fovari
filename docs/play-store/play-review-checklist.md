# Google Play readiness checklist

- [ ] Finalize company, privacy, support, deletion, and terms URLs.
- [ ] Complete target audience and Families Policy review.
- [ ] Complete Data safety from observed production SDK/network behavior.
- [ ] Configure Play Billing products for digital subscriptions.
- [ ] Prove purchase, acknowledgment, restore, renewal, grace, pause, cancel, and refund in sandbox.
- [ ] Verify account and family deletion paths are reachable inside and outside the app.
- [ ] Test phone, 7-inch tablet, 10-inch tablet, portrait/landscape, TalkBack, font scaling, and
      reduced animation.
- [ ] Review advertising ID, analytics, crash, push, and provider SDK collection.
- [ ] Complete content rating, app access, ads declaration, and child-safety answers.
- [ ] Run pre-launch report and resolve security, accessibility, and compatibility findings.
- [ ] Archive signed bundle, mapping/symbol files, SBOM, screenshots, declarations, and review
      result.

The local build uses no real billing, advertising, production analytics, or production child data.
